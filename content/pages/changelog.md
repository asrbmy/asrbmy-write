## 2026-10-17 — Footnotes, feeds and friendlier reading

- Footnotes, a floating Contents button on phones, copy-a-link for any heading, and a slideshow view of any article.
- Feeds for every tag and category, plus Atom and JSON Feed.
- A Start here page, short Notes, Now and Uses pages, reviews with star ratings, audio posts with chapters, and a "What changed" log on articles.
- "Was this helpful?" buttons, "Read" marks and a reading streak (kept only in your own browser), and export/import for the reading list.
- Automatic accessibility and colour-contrast checks on every build.

## 2026-10-15 — More ways to read and share

- Share, copy-link, save-for-later and print buttons on every article.
- A reading list that lives in your own browser.
- Glossary, projects, "Best of" and author pages.
- Diagrams, maths and click-to-play videos in articles.
- Translations with a language switcher.

## 2026-10-14 — Series, covers and comments

- Multi-part series, cover images and a reading-progress bar.
- Scheduled posts and optional GitHub-based comments.
